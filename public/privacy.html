Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Norra AI Platform (mynorra.xyz)

1. Information We Collect

Scores by Norra AI collects minimal data necessary to provide reliable live match services, commercial API access, and customer support:

  • Account & Developer Information: When you register on our developer portal or sign in via Google One-Tap/OAuth, we store your full name, email address, password hash (for password accounts), and Google avatar/account ID (for Google accounts).
  • API Usage & Telemetry: Request counters, IP addresses, user agent headers, and API key metrics to monitor fair use, prevent abuse, and enforce rate limits.
  • Transaction Records: For paid plan subscribers, we store transaction identifiers, payment gateway references, plan tier, amount, and subscription renewal status. Financial card numbers are processed directly by Flutterwave and PayPal and are never stored on our servers.
  • Local Device Preferences: We use browser localStorage to remember your selected theme (dark or light mode) and mute/sound preferences for live score alerts.

2. How We Use Information

We use your collected information strictly to:

  • Authenticate your account and issue active commercial API keys.
  • Send transactional notifications, email verification links, and subscription receipts.
  • Prevent denial-of-service attacks, fraudulent API key generation, and excessive unauthorized rate bursts.
  • Operate the Norra AI platform and provide customer assistance.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or API usage data to third-party data brokers.

3. Third-Party Services & Integrations

Our platform interfaces with select high-trust third-party service providers:

  • Google Identity Services: Used for optional Google Sign-in and registration. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Payment Processors (Flutterwave & PayPal): Process payments and handle recurring billing according to international PCI-DSS compliance standards.
  • Cloudflare: Acts as our global reverse proxy and content delivery network (CDN) for DDoS mitigation, caching, and SSL encryption.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Secure cloud database infrastructure hosted with industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We do not use invasive third-party cross-site behavioral tracking cookies. We utilize privacy-preserving, cookieless analytics for aggregate page view statistics to optimize server performance and bandwidth allocation.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your account and generated API keys at any time through the Developer Portal or by emailing our data protection officer at support@mynorra.xyz.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. Updated versions will be posted on this page with an updated timestamp.
