Official Platform: scores.mynorra.xyz (Scores by Norra AI) • Part of the Norra AI Ecosystem (mynorra.xyz)
Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 2.1

1. Platform Scope & Information We Collect

Scores by Norra AI operates transparently under the domain scores.mynorra.xyz. We collect only minimal, purposeful information required to provide reliable live match services, commercial API access, and customer support:

  • Developer & Account Credentials: When creating an account or authenticating via Google One-Tap/OAuth, we store your full name, email address, password hash (for direct signups), and Google avatar/account ID. We never store raw plain-text passwords.
  • API Usage Telemetry: Request counters, endpoint access stamps, user-agent signatures, and API key metrics used solely for enforcing fair-use rate limits and monitoring server health.
  • Payment Transaction Records: For commercial subscribers (Starter, Pro, and Whitelabel tiers — see Pricing), we record transaction references, plan status, and payment gateway tokens. Credit card details are handled directly by PCI-DSS certified gateways (Flutterwave & PayPal) and never touch our servers.
  • Browser Preferences: We use client-side localStorage solely to remember your chosen theme (dark/light) and audio preference for goal alerts.

2. How We Use Your Information

All information collected on scores.mynorra.xyz is used exclusively for:

  • Authenticating developers and provisioning secure API credentials.
  • Transmitting transactional notifications, email verification tokens, and billing confirmations.
  • Safeguarding our infrastructure against DDoS attacks, automated scraping, and unauthorized API key abuse.
  • Delivering high-availability livescore feeds to visitors and business partners.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or API usage metadata to third-party ad networks or data brokers.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure & Security

We partner only with industry-leading, high-trust providers to ensure robust data security:

  • Google Identity Services: Provides optional, one-click OAuth authentication adhering to strict Google privacy standards.
  • Payment Processors (Flutterwave & PayPal): Process all global transactions in compliance with international financial and banking privacy regulations.
  • Cloudflare & CDN: Delivers global TLS 1.3 encryption, DDoS mitigation, and edge caching for sub-millisecond responses.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Secure cloud database infrastructure hosted in SOC 2 Type II compliant facilities with encryption both in transit and at rest.

4. Cookies & Analytics

Scores by Norra AI utilizes privacy-focused, cookieless analytics to monitor aggregate server latency, request rates, and platform stability without identifying or tracking individual human visitors. Client-side local storage (localStorage) is used strictly for storing non-sensitive user preferences such as your active theme (dark/light) and audio toggle state for live goal alerts.

5. Third-Party Advertising, Promotional Sponsors & Betting Partners

To support our free tier infrastructure and high-throughput livescore polling services, scores.mynorra.xyz may display contextual advertisements, bookmaker promotional banners, sponsor placements, and universal betting code conversion widgets.

  • Partner Links & Referral Tracking: When you click on a sponsored banner, bookmaker promotion, or copy/load a converted betting slip code (e.g. SportyBet, Bet9ja), the destination partner may use anonymized session cookies, referral parameters, or server tags to verify affiliate attribution and registration eligibility.
  • No PII Shared: We never sell, transmit, or share your personally identifiable information (such as your account email, hashed password, API keys, or payment billing records) with advertising or sports betting partners.
  • Ad-Free Experience: Subscribers to our commercial API tiers (Starter, Pro, and Whitelabel) enjoy an entirely ad-free developer environment and embeddable widget options with zero sponsor branding.
  • User Privacy Controls & Opt-Outs: You can manage or disable cookies through your web browser preferences or by enabling Global Privacy Control (GPC) and Do Not Track (DNT) headers. For third-party ad networks, you may also opt out of interest-based ads via industry programs such as the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) and Your Online Choices.
  • Age Requirement (18+): Betting-related promotional content, bookmaker tools, and odds integrations are intended strictly for users aged 18 and older (or the applicable legal age of majority in your jurisdiction). Scores by Norra AI encourages responsible gaming.

6. Data Retention & User Rights

You have full ownership and control over your personal data. You may review your active API keys, update account settings, or request account and data deletion at any time directly through the Developer Portal or by emailing our privacy team at support@mynorra.xyz.

7. Contact Us

For inquiries regarding privacy, data protection, advertising partnerships, or compliance, please reach out to our team at support@mynorra.xyz or explore Norra AI at mynorra.xyz.
