1. Platform Scope & Information We Collect

Scores by Norra AI operates transparently under the domain scores.mynorra.xyz. We collect only minimal, purposeful information required to provide reliable live match services, commercial API access, and customer support:

  • Developer & Account Credentials: When creating an account or authenticating via Google One-Tap/OAuth, we store your full name, email address, password hash (for direct signups), and Google avatar/account ID. We never store raw plain-text passwords.
  • API Usage Telemetry: Request counters, endpoint access stamps, user-agent signatures, and API key metrics used solely for enforcing fair-use rate limits and monitoring server health.
  • Payment Transaction Records: For commercial subscribers ($10 Starter, $29 Pro, $79 Whitelabel), we record transaction references, plan status, and payment gateway tokens. Credit card details are handled directly by PCI-DSS certified gateways (Flutterwave & PayPal) and never touch our servers.
  • Browser Preferences: We use client-side localStorage solely to remember your chosen theme (dark/light) and audio preference for goal alerts.

2. How We Use Your Information

All information collected on scores.mynorra.xyz is used exclusively for:

  • Authenticating developers and provisioning secure API credentials.
  • Transmitting transactional notifications, email verification tokens, and billing confirmations.
  • Safeguarding our infrastructure against DDoS attacks, automated scraping, and unauthorized API key abuse.
  • Delivering high-availability livescore feeds to visitors and business partners.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or API usage metadata to third-party ad networks or data brokers.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure & Security

We partner only with industry-leading, high-trust providers to ensure robust data security:

  • Google Identity Services: Provides optional, one-click OAuth authentication adhering to strict Google privacy standards.
  • Payment Processors (Flutterwave & PayPal): Process all global transactions in compliance with international financial and banking privacy regulations.
  • Cloudflare & CDN: Delivers global TLS 1.3 encryption, DDoS mitigation, and edge caching for sub-millisecond responses.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Secure cloud database infrastructure hosted in SOC 2 Type II compliant facilities with encryption both in transit and at rest.

4. Cookies & Analytics

Scores by Norra AI does not deploy cross-site tracking cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or third-party profiling scripts. We utilize privacy-preserving, cookieless analytics to monitor aggregate traffic patterns and server load without identifying individual visitors.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

You have full ownership and rights over your data. You may review your active API keys, update account settings, or request account and data deletion at any time directly through the Developer Portal or by emailing our privacy team at support@mynorra.xyz.

6. Contact Us

For inquiries regarding privacy, data protection, or compliance, please reach out to our team at support@mynorra.xyz or explore Norra AI at mynorra.xyz.
