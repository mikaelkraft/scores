Frequently Asked Questions

What are the exact API rate limits and quotas?

Free Developer: 120 req/min (100 req/hr sandbox) for development across free tier leagues.
Starter (Starter Tier): 500 req/min (1,000 req/hr) across all 100+ global leagues with booking conversions.
Pro (Pro Tier): 1,500 req/min (10,000 req/hr) with full H2H statistics, Norra AI prediction context, and live webhooks.
Enterprise (Enterprise Tier): 5,000 req/min (high throughput) with ML streaming and whitelabeling.

How does Norra AI ingest live football and prediction data?

Norra AI syncs directly through our dedicated ML endpoints (/api/v1/football/predictions-context/:eventId and /api/v1/football/training-data) using the internal Master Admin key. Data ingested includes lifetime H2H records, 6-game team form guides, goal scoring averages, clean sheet rates, and in-play telemetry to generate deep-dive predictions.

How quickly do I get my API key?

Instantly. Register for free in the Developer Portal, and your key is generated immediately without any waiting period.

What payment gateways are supported?

We accept credit and debit cards worldwide via PayPal and Flutterwave, as well as local bank transfers, mobile money, and USSD in supported regions.

What is the Universal Booking Code Converter?

A universal API service that lets your users paste a bet code from one bookmaker (e.g., SportyBet) and instantly re-map it to another (Bet9ja, 1xBet, Bet365, Betway, Stake, Betfair, DraftKings, FanDuel) with matched event, market, and selection identifiers.

Which leagues are included in the Free tier?

The Free Developer tier includes top leagues for sandbox integration (Premier League, La Liga, NPFL). Paid tiers (Starter, Pro, Enterprise) unlock all 100+ global competitions, including African leagues (CAF Champions League, AFCON, PSL, Ghana Premier League, Kenya Premier League), South American leagues, and cup knockout brackets.

Can I cancel or upgrade my plan anytime?

Yes, there are no long-term contracts. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time directly inside your account dashboard.

How does the SSE Delta Stream save battery and data?

Our real-time SSE stream transmits lightweight delta packets (~120 bytes per change) rather than full match objects. Mobile devices automatically back off or pause connections when the browser tab is backgrounded, reducing data consumption by up to 95%.
