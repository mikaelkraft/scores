Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly do I get my API key?

Instantly. Register for free in the Developer Portal, and your key is generated immediately without any waiting period.

What payment gateways are supported?

We accept credit and debit cards worldwide via PayPal and Flutterwave, as well as local bank transfers, mobile money, and USSD in supported regions.

What is the Booking Code Converter?

A universal API service that lets your users paste a bet code from one bookmaker (e.g., SportyBet) and instantly re-map it to another (Bet9ja, 1xBet, Betway, Mozzart) with matched event and market identifiers.

Can I cancel or upgrade my plan anytime?

Yes, there are no long-term contracts. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time directly inside your account dashboard.
